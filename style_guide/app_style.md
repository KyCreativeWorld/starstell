# App Style

## Button and Window Style

Buttons and windows should have a glassy, slightly transparent look to make it feel more modern and space-esque.

Buttons should be rounded slightly to give the app a soft and approachable feel.

## Background Style

The background and main page, for the lavender_space theme, should be primarily black with tiny white dots (stars) spread across the background. These dots should blink slightly (fade from bright white to a fainter white and back).
