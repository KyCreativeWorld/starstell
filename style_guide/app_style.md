# App Style

## Button and Window Style

Buttons and windows should have a glassy, slightly transparent look to make it feel more modern and space-esque.

Buttons should be rounded slightly to give the app a soft and approachable feel.

## Background Style

The background and main page, for the lavender_space theme, should be primarily black with tiny white dots (stars) spread across the background. These dots should blink slightly (fade from bright white to a fainter white and back).

## Light Calm Theme

A clean, flat take on Cyan: quiet porcelain background, solid white surfaces and a refined cyan. No glass.

| Role | Color | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Background | `#f6f8f9` cool porcelain | Page background and input fills |
| Surface | `#ffffff` white | Cards, panels and popup menus |
| Border | `#e1e8ea` hairline grey | 1px outlines on cards, panels and menus |
| Text | `#1d2a2f` deep slate | All body text and the STARSTELL wordmark |
| Muted text | `#5f6f75` slate grey | Secondary labels and hints |
| Primary | `#1f97ad` cyan | Icons, links and selected states |
| Accent | `#0f6b7c` deep cyan | Filled buttons (with white text) and emphasis |

- **Background:** no stars and no glows; a plain, even color.
- **Buttons and windows:** solid and flat, with no frosted blur, sheen or glossy reflection. Windows are white with a hairline border and a very soft shadow. Filled buttons use the deep cyan so their labels stay crisp.
- **Font:** Trebuchet, with the Didot wordmark, the same as the other themes.
## Cyan Space Theme

Identical to Light Calm (same colors, flat surfaces and cyan sidebar), with one difference: chart graphics look like pieces of the night sky.

- **Charts:** bars, pie slices and the area under line graphs are filled near-black (`#0b111e`, with slightly lighter navy shades to tell pie slices apart). Very small white stars (about 1px) are scattered inside each mark and twinkle in staggered waves. Lines themselves are black. Secondary series (e.g. upfront cost next to savings) use slate grey `#7d8796`.
- **Everything else:** no star field in the page background and no glass; the page stays clean and corporate.
